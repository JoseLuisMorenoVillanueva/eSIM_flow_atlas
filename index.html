Scope and reading notes

Diagrams show LPAd and IPAd so device-to-eUICC interfaces are explicit. LPAe and IPAe reside inside the eUICC; do not interpret their internal interactions as the same external device interface. Steps group protocol exchanges and omit many conditional branches. A numbered step here is a learning step, not the standard’s step number.

SGP.32 v1.3 refers to a specific SGP.22 baseline in its own references; the SGP.22 v3.1 tab is a separate reference, not a claim that all v3.1 features apply to SGP.32. Profile state changes and cellular registration are separate layers. These diagrams do not simulate a real eUICC or prove device compliance.
